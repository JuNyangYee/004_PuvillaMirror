# 노을 마을 광장

사용자 레퍼런스의 남보라 하늘, 코럴 노을, 호박색 창문, 라벤더 눈밭을 유지하면서 같은 마을의 다른 장소로 생성했다. 왼쪽 카페·종탑, 오른쪽 골목, 중앙 눈 광장 구성이다. 캐릭터와 전경 담장은 배경에 포함하지 않았다.

에셋: public/bg/winter-village-square-sunset.png
생성 방식: 내장 image_gen, 레퍼런스는 색감·재질·세계관 참고용.
이전 public/bg/winter-village-reference.png는 그대로 보관한다.

유화 활성화 상태에서 확인한 조명: key 1.9 (#fff6ee, -38°/32°), fill 1.4 (#c5cae6, 55°/22°), rim 3.6 (#ffbd7d, -125°/24°), bounce 0.32 (#dfcddd, 15°/-32°). hemi 0.9, sky #b8c2e6 / ground #d9c4da, env 0.07, exposure 1.02. 현재 갤러리 유화 수치는 유지한다.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: full bleed 9:16 portrait background plate for an interactive white bear scene. Input image is ONLY A COLOR / MATERIAL / WORLD REFERENCE, not a layout reference. Design a DISTINCT NEW CAMERA LOCATION elsewhere in this same cozy snowy alpine Christmas village. NEW COMPOSITION: looking across a quiet small village square from its open snowy southern edge; in the left middle distance a modest chalet cafe with a low broad roof and two glowing arched windows; on the right middle distance a pair of narrower offset timber houses receding into a gently curving lane; a slender village bell tower stands LEFT of center far behind them; a small fir tree dressed with warm fairy lights stands at the FAR RIGHT edge. Asymmetric varied rooflines, no giant close-up triangular gable filling the left or right side. Distant snowy peaks only softly visible between buildings, NOT a large panoramic mountain range spanning the center. Upper third: deep indigo-violet twilight sky with a few sweeping coral sunset clouds, warm orange glow near the far horizon. Lower 35 percent: a continuous open gently textured snowy plaza with lavender blue shadows and soft peach gold reflection; no walls, no steps across front, no foreground fence. Keep central 45 percent relatively calm and clear so a separately rendered 3D bear can stand there. Match the reference's refined cinematic storybook 3D miniature wood/stone/snow materials, warm amber windows, strong warm/cool contrast. Golden illumination from back-left with cool lavender ambient sky. Absolutely no people or animals or characters, no text/watermarks. No dense snow particles; app adds snowfall. This must clearly look like a DIFFERENT STREET/SQUARE in the same world, not the same scene with characters removed. Portrait 9:16 high resolution.
